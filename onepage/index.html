1Before you play laptop

  1. Join the XR18 Wi-Fi.
  2. Open XR18 Rehearsal. Press Find. The dot turns green.
  3. Press Check mixer. An empty list is good. Otherwise press Sync & check.
  4. Pick today's players under Who is playing.
  5. Room mics plugged in? Tick channels 15-16.

Gain is set per input in X AIR Edit. The computer outputs to USB 1-2. At the end, close the app normally.

2Who hears what

Duet ("Both"): Ben on Aux 5, Barak on Aux 6. The backing track and E-Pad are in every mix.

3Swapping a player

  • Click the new name. The app does the rest.
  • It saves the old player's mix and loads the new one's.
  • Swap back and the first mix returns exactly.
  • Nobody else's mix changes.

4Your own mix

  • Change it in the X AIR app on your phone or tablet.
  • Touch only your own aux sends.
  • It is saved on its own: every 10 s, on every swap, and when the app closes. No Save button.
  • Drummer: your mix is the channel faders. Everyone else leaves them alone.

5Mute groups and room mics

Mute groups: 1 Talkback & Amb · 2 Drums · 3 Guitar · 4 Bass. Unmute and it is exactly as before. Singers are in none.

Room mics (15-16): tick them on. 48V and a 2 s wait are automatic, so there is no pop.

Mixer not answering

The dot is red. Check the laptop is on the XR18 Wi-Fi, then press Find. A swap waits and loads by itself when the mixer answers.

No sound in your ears

  • Is your mute group off?
  • Is your channel ticked?
  • Is your pack on your jack, volume up?
  • Then press Check mixer.

Wrong mix or odd settings

Adjust your sends in X AIR. It is saved. If a setting was changed by hand, press Check mixer, then Sync & check. Set up mixer… is for the first time, or after a scene reset.

Recording

Pick the XR18 as the DAW interface (18 inputs). Windows needs the Behringer X-USB ASIO driver. Record the backing track inside the DAW.
